📏 Size & Measurement Guide

Choose a standard size or provide your own measurements for a more customized fit.

✨ Custom Measurements

Custom measurements are available for our products. Please provide your measurements when discussing your order.

📐 Common Measurements

  • Chest / Bust: Measure around the fullest part of the bust.
  • Waist: Measure around the natural waist.
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of the hips.
  • Shoulder: Measure from one shoulder to the other across the back.
  • Sleeve Length: Measure from the shoulder to your desired sleeve length.
  • Garment Length: Measure from the top of the shoulder to your desired length.

💡 Need Help?

If you are unsure about your measurements, contact us and we will help you with the details.
