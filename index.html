About Dream Designs Biz

Welcome to Dream Designs Biz — where every thread tells a beautiful story. We create and customize sarees, lehengas, dresses, kurtis, anarkalis, designer blouses, and more to match your style, measurements, and occasion. Choose your preferred colour, fabric, design, and detailing, and let us create something special just for you.

Our mission is to bring your fashion dreams to life with creativity, craftsmanship, and personal attention. Whether you are looking for a timeless saree transformation, a beautiful lehenga, a stylish kurti, an elegant anarkali, or a perfectly customized blouse, we are here to create something that feels special and uniquely yours.

📏 Size & Measurement Guide

Choose a standard size or provide your own measurements for a more customized fit.

✨ Custom Measurements

Custom measurements are available for our products. Please provide your measurements when discussing your order.

📐 Common Measurements

  • Chest / Bust: Measure around the fullest part of the bust.
  • Waist: Measure around the natural waist.
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of the hips.
  • Shoulder: Measure from one shoulder to the other across the back.
  • Sleeve Length: Measure from the shoulder to your desired sleeve length.
  • Garment Length: Measure from the top of the shoulder to your desired length.
